What This Is

Couch to First Stripe is a beginner-friendly introduction to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, built for adults who've never trained before. Inspired by programs like Couch to 5K, it takes you from zero experience through the real foundations of the art: safe falling, escapes, positional control, and mat awareness, while building genuine fitness along the way.

Every class is built for newcomers. No experience required, no prior athleticism assumed, no ego at the door. Come curious. Leave capable.

The Path

How It Works

01

Move Safely

Learn to fall, roll, and shrimp — the physical basics everyone starts with, before anything else.

02

Hold Your Ground

Build the posture and base that keep you from getting stuck, on top or on bottom.

03

Escape Anything

Get out of mount and side control, even under real pressure.

04

Defend Your Guard

Keep control from the bottom, no matter who's on top.

05

Completion Day

Show what you've built, live, and earn your certificate of completion.

Schedule & Registration

  • Schedule

    Two separate 8-week courses, Mondays and Wednesdays, one hour each. Session 1 begins in January; Session 2 begins in March. Each course stands alone, so you can join at the start of either one.

  • Try a Class

    Not ready for eight weeks? A single-class drop-in is available. Bring your gi.

  • Who It's For

    Adults 18+. No experience needed.

  • Register

    Registration opens Tuesday, Dec. 8 at 10 a.m. through the City of Escondido, which handles registration, payment, and waivers.

What You'll Need

  • Required

    A gi (kimono), required for every class, including a drop-in. A simple, beginner-friendly option will be shared with registered students before the first class.

  • Bring

    A water bottle and a towel, the same basics you'd bring to the gym, plus comfortable athletic wear to train under your gi.

  • Not Required

    Any prior experience, athleticism, or special equipment. Every black belt started exactly where you are.

The Payoff

Completion Day

Every course ends the same way. It's not a test you cram for — it's a chance to show, live, that the fundamentals you built over eight weeks actually hold up. You leave with a certificate of completion, real skills you can rely on, and a clear sense of what comes next. Your first stripe is a later milestone on the path, and you'll be ready to keep working toward it.

Meet Your Instructor

Mark Reyes

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Brown Belt · Gracie Barra Escondido

Mark has trained in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu since 2017. He spent five years at Ribeiro Jiu-Jitsu Carlsbad, going from white belt to purple belt. Since 2022, he has trained at Gracie Barra Escondido, where he now holds the rank of brown belt.

He built Couch to First Stripe for adults who want more from a workout — real stress relief, practical self-defense, personal growth, or just an affordable way to stay active. You do not need experience. You do not need to be young or in great shape. Every class starts with the basics, taught step by step. You walk in without fear, and you walk out with real skills — and the confidence to keep going.
