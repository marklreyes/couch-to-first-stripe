What This Is

Couch to First Stripe is a beginner-friendly introduction to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, built for adults who've never trained before. Inspired by programs like Couch to 5K, it takes you from zero experience through the real foundations of the art: safe falling, escapes, positional control, and mat awareness, while building genuine fitness along the way.

Every class is built for newcomers. No gear required to start, no prior athleticism assumed, no ego at the door. Come curious. Leave capable.

The Path

How It Works

01

Move Safely

Learn to fall, roll, and shrimp — the physical basics everyone starts with, before anything else.

02

Hold Your Ground

Build the posture and base that keep you from getting stuck, on top or on bottom.

03

Escape Anything

Get out of mount and side control, even under real pressure.

04

Defend Your Guard

Keep control from the bottom, no matter who's on top.

05

Stripe Day

Show what you've built, live, and earn your first stripe.

What You'll Need

  • Required

    A gi (kimono). A simple, beginner-friendly option will be shared with enrolled students before the first class.

  • Bring

    Comfortable athletic wear to train underneath.

  • Not Required

    Any prior experience, athleticism, or gear beyond the gi. Everyone starts on the couch.

The Payoff

Stripe Day

Every season ends the same way. It's not a test you cram for — it's a chance to show, live, that the fundamentals you built over the past several weeks actually hold up. When your instructor ties that first stripe onto your belt, it means one thing: you're no longer on the couch.
