Bring Beginner Jiu-Jitsu to Your Community

Couch to First Stripe is a beginner-only Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu course for adults who have never trained. It's built to fit a recreation catalog: fixed-length sessions, a clear finish line, and an experienced instructor who runs the curriculum.

Why It Fits

A Course Your Catalog Can Run

  • Built for Beginners

    Adults 18+, no experience needed. Every class is designed for newcomers, and the first thing students learn is how to move and fall safely.

  • Catalog-Ready Format

    Standalone 8-week sessions, two one-hour classes a week. Each session stands on its own, so new students can join at the start of any one.

  • An Easy First Step

    A single-class drop-in lets curious residents try one class before committing to a full session.

  • A Clear Finish Line

    Every session ends with Completion Day and a certificate of completion, a concrete outcome students can work toward and you can describe in your listing.
