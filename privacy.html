Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026
Note to self before publishing: this is a plain-language starting draft, not legal advice. Couch to First Stripe ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you sign up for updates or send us a partner inquiry, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What We Collect

  • Your email address (required to sign up).
  • Your first name, if you choose to provide it.
  • Basic email engagement data (such as opens and clicks), collected automatically by our email service provider so we know which updates are useful.

We do not collect payment information, physical addresses, or any other personal details through this signup form.

How We Use It

We use your information only to:

  • Remind you when registration opens for upcoming courses.
  • Send occasional emails about the program — what to expect, updates, and related content.

We do not use your information for anything beyond communicating with you about Couch to First Stripe.

Partner Inquiries

If you contact us through our partner page on behalf of an organization, we collect what you enter in that form: your name, work email, organization, organization type, city and state, and optionally your role, phone number, timeline, and message. We also record how you found the page (such as a campaign link or referring website). We use this only to respond to your inquiry and discuss a possible partnership. It is not added to our student email list.

Who We Share It With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party. We use MailerLite as our email service provider to store your information and send communications on our behalf. Your data is subject to MailerLite's own privacy and security practices as our data processor. Partner inquiries are received and stored by Netlify, our website host, on the same basis.

Course registration, payment, and waivers are handled by the City of Escondido under its own policies. We do not receive that information.

Site Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site. It uses cookies to collect anonymized usage data, such as the pages you visit, how you arrived, and your device and browser type. We use this only to improve the site and see how people find the program. You can opt out with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your Choices

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time, and we'll stop sending you messages. If you'd like your information removed sooner, contact us directly using the details below.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, your information is removed from active use within [X days/at your next unsubscribe request].

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach out at couchtofirststripe@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be posted at this page.
